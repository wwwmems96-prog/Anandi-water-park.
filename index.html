A place made for happy families

Anandi Water Park began with one simple wish: to give families a place where children laugh out loud, parents finally relax, and grandparents smile at the sound of splashing water.

Our water park brings together thrilling slides, a wave pool and a gentle kids zone, all watched over by trained lifeguards and kept spotless every single day.

When the fun is over, our resort rooms offer a quiet, comfortable stay with fresh food and warm hospitality, just like home.

Our lawns and halls are also home to weddings, engagements, birthdays and corporate gatherings, planned with care so that you can enjoy your own celebration.

Every guest is treated like family, and every memory made here is one we are proud to be part of. We would love to welcome you next.
